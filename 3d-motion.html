01The problem

Making things look real without a studio.

Jewelry needs exact measurements and believable metal, and a product ad needs a story. Doing either by hand, frame by frame, would take weeks.

02What I built

Everything is generated by scripts. The clover bracelet is built in Blender with Python: five motifs, 42 cable links that thread without touching, and a working clasp, rendered in Cycles. The ads run a canvas animation through headless Chrome, composite it onto a 3D laptop in Blender and add a synthesized soundtrack and narration.

  • AParametric bracelet and ring models with real dimensions
  • BCycles renders with studio lighting
  • CCode-rendered ads: canvas → Blender → narrated video

04Result

Renders that pass for product photos.

Changing a size or a motif is an edit and a re-run, not a remodel.

What I learned: a story beats effects. The first ad was a reel of animations; the one that worked told one small story.
