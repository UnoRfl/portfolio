Mochi Print

A cute web app that prints photos, notes and doodles to a tiny Bluetooth thermal printer.

Role
Design & development
Year
2026
Tools
Web Bluetooth · Canvas
Link
unorfl.github.io/mochi-print ↗

01The problem

A tiny printer that deserved a friendlier app.

A kids’ instant-print camera came with a clunky companion app. A family member wanted to print photos and little notes from their phone without fighting it.

02What I built

A single-page web app that talks to the printer directly over Web Bluetooth. I worked out the printer’s protocol packet by packet, then built photo, note and doodle modes with frames and captions. Images are dithered on a canvas down to the printer’s 384-pixel line.

  • ASpeaks the printer’s Bluetooth protocol directly
  • BPhoto, note and doodle modes with frames
  • CWorks on iPhone through a Bluetooth browser

04Result

Prints from a phone in a couple of taps.

No install and no account: open the page, connect, print.

What I learned: hardware lies. The printer reported "no paper" when it was really in webcam mode, and every print came out upside down until I flipped the image.
