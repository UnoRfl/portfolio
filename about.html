About

I like building things that leave the screen.

I’m Juan Rafael, though most people online know me as Uno. I’m a student who is still figuring out what I want to be great at, and I’m enjoying every step of finding out.

I learn best by making things. When something catches my eye, whether it’s a website, a game or a little gadget, I would rather try building it myself than just read about it.

Every project pushes me a bit further than the last. I’m always looking for something new to try, and new ways to expand my horizons.
