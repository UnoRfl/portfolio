TPN OS

A point-of-sale, kitchen and invoicing system built to replace a paid POS at a real restaurant.

Role
Design & development
Year
2026
Tools
Supabase · Android · ESC/POS
Link
unorfl.github.io/tpn-os ↗

01The problem

Orders, the kitchen and the paperwork all lived in different places.

The restaurant was paying a yearly fee for a POS it barely used, and invoices were written by hand from a booklet only when a customer asked. The owners needed one system the counter staff could learn in minutes, and one they could afford to keep.

02What I built

A Supabase-backed web app that runs on the restaurant’s iMin counter terminals through two small Android apps I wrote, TPN Counter and TPN Kitchen. Every order prints a receipt for the guest and a ticket on the kitchen’s network printer, guests can order from a QR menu at their table, and the counter keeps taking orders when the internet drops.

  • ACounter and kitchen apps on iMin terminals
  • BReceipts and kitchen tickets over ESC/POS
  • CQR table menu, offline mode and BIR-ready invoices

04Result

A POS the restaurant owns, not rents.

No yearly licence fee, and every money rule is guarded by 220 automated database checks.

What I learned: real users find every edge case within a week. Building for a busy counter taught me to make the common action one tap and keep everything else out of the way.
